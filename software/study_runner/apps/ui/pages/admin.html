     Research-grade biosignal workflow
    
              
    Study Runner is a research tool. It is not a medical device and produces no diagnosis.
    
            
    
            
    
              
     Sensors
    
              
    EEG, heart and breathing rate, and camera-based emotion estimates are recorded as separate streams and never merged into a single score.
    
            
    
            
    
              
     Timing
    
              
    LSL timestamps are the reference clock. Tablet events carry their own clock offset so browser and sensor time can be compared honestly.
    
            
    
            
    
              
     Data
    
              
    Raw values are kept. Camera frames are analysed and discarded; no video is ever stored. Results are written crash-safe before any upload is attempted.
    
            
    
            
    
              
     Limits
    
              
    Emotion estimates are model output, not ground truth. Wireless sensors can drop samples; gaps are recorded rather than interpolated.
    
            
    
            
    
              
     Lab workflow
    
              
    
                
    1
    Check every sensor on the dashboard before the participant arrives.
    
                
    2
    Load the study, select a waiting device if several are connected, then press Play.
    
                
    3
    Watch the live status during the session; interruptions are recorded, not hidden.
    
                
    4
    After the session, verify the saved files and the upload state before clearing the room.

     The exported study file
    
              
    The file contains the questions, plugin selections, and non-secret destination settings so the study can run on another computer. Credentials stay in the local secret store.
    
              
    Some destination settings, such as public share links, may grant access even without a separate password. Share study files only with authorized people.
